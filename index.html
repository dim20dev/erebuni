Our Ethos

Craft, Fire & Warmth

At Erebuni, we honor ancient Armenian culinary traditions with the bounty of Cypriot land. Every dish is an ode to slow living — from wood-fired lavash to slow-cooked lamb shoulder, using organic produce and coastal inspirations.

Chef Aram Manukyan brings 20 years of heritage-led cooking, creating a menu that whispers elegance and roars authenticity. Our terrace overlooks the gentle Napa breeze, while candlelight defines every evening.
