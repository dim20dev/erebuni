Welcome to Erebuni

Nestled in vibrant Ayia Napa, Erebuni brings the rich flavors of Armenia and the Mediterranean together. From traditional kebabs and dolma to fresh seafood and fine wines — every dish tells a story of warmth, heritage, and passion. Enjoy breathtaking sunsets, cozy ambience, and exceptional service.
