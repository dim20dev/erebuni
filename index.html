Our Philosophy

Heritage on a Plate

Erebuni is more than a restaurant — it's a love letter to Armenian culinary traditions, reimagined with Mediterranean soul. Chef Aram Manukyan brings generations of family recipes, using only the finest seasonal ingredients from Cypriot farmers and the surrounding waters.

Every dish tells a story: from hand-rolled manti to slow-roasted lamb, from wild herbs to artisanal wines. Our open kitchen invites you to witness the craftsmanship behind each plate.
